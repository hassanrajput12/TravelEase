🎒 Packing Essentials

Clothing

  • Warm jacket (temperatures drop at night)

  • Hat and sunglasses

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wi-Fi available in Northern Pakistan?

Wi-Fi is available in major towns and hotels, but can be slow and unreliable in remote areas. Mobile data coverage is limited in mountainous regions. Download offline maps and important information before traveling.

Do I need a guide for trekking?

While not mandatory for all areas, a local guide is highly recommended for safety, navigation, and cultural insights. Required for certain restricted areas and technical treks. Guides also help with language barriers and local customs.

What's the best time to visit?

May to September is the peak tourist season with the best weather. April and October offer fewer crowds but unpredictable weather. Winter (November-March) is extremely cold with many roads closed.

Are there ATMs in remote areas?

ATMs are scarce in remote areas. Withdraw sufficient cash in major cities like Islamabad, Gilgit, or Skardu. Many places only accept cash payments, so plan accordingly.

Is it safe for solo female travelers?

Northern Pakistan is generally safe, but solo female travelers should exercise caution. Join group tours when possible, dress modestly, and inform someone of your travel plans. Local women can provide valuable advice and support.

What about altitude sickness?

Altitude sickness can occur above 2,500m. Ascend gradually, stay hydrated, and carry medication. Symptoms include headache, nausea, and fatigue. Descend immediately if symptoms worsen.
